Privacy Policy

What this website collects when you reach out, which services help me run it, and how to ask me to see or delete your information.

This site exists so you can learn about Hawaiʻi Island and reach me when you're ready to talk. It collects only what you send me and the usual visit statistics. Here is the plain-English version.

Who I am

Keala M. Wall, REALTOR® (Hawaiʻi license RS-82797), with The Agency Team Hawaiʻi, brokered by eXp Realty (brokerage license RB-21841), 6600 Kalanianaʻole Hwy #114, Honolulu, HI 96825. You can reach me at Keala@kealawall.com or 808-515-3081.

What this site collects

  • What you send me. The contact form asks for your name, phone, email, what you're hoping to do and a short message. The free checklist and fact-sheet forms ask for your name and email. Anything you email, text or send on Instagram comes to me the same way.
  • Visit statistics. The site uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies and records pages viewed, how you arrived, your general location and your device and browser type. It does not tell me who you are. You can opt out with Google's browser add-on or by blocking cookies. The site doesn't respond to browser Do Not Track signals.
  • Technical details. Like any website, the host (Netlify) sees your IP address and browser details in its server logs.

How I use it

To reply, to send the guide you asked for, and to follow up on your question. When you request a checklist or fact sheet, I may also send you my occasional email updates about Hawaiʻi Island property; every email has an unsubscribe link, and replying "unsubscribe" works too. I don't sell your information or share it for anyone else's marketing.

Who helps run this site

  • Netlify hosts the site and delivers form submissions.
  • Zapier moves each submission into my contact system.
  • Follow Up Boss is the contact system my brokerage team uses to keep track of conversations and send email updates.
  • Google Analytics provides the visit statistics above, and Google Fonts supplies the site's typefaces (your browser loads them from Google).
  • Touchpoint AI builds and maintains this website and can see submissions and site statistics only as needed to keep it running.

Links to other sites (my brokerage's listing search, MLS listings, Instagram, county and state pages) take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

Keeping, seeing and deleting your information

I keep inquiries as long as our conversation is active and as long as Hawaiʻi real estate record-keeping rules require for client files. Email me at Keala@kealawall.com to see, correct or delete what I have about you, and I'll take care of it unless a record has to be kept by law.

Children

This site is for adults and isn't directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the date at the top will change with it.
